# Evidence roadmap: O-1A and EB-1A

Prepared September 9, 2026. Private working notes, not website copy. This is an evidence-development plan based on the supplied portfolio and CV, not a determination of eligibility. EB-1 includes multiple routes; this plan assumes EB-1A extraordinary ability. Have qualified US immigration counsel assess the complete record and appropriate route before filing.

## Priority

Make your contribution and its independent significance easy to establish. A self-authored website helps people find the record; it does not independently establish acclaim, satisfy a criterion, or guarantee approval. Do not treat three apparent evidence categories as an approval forecast.

The materials suggest a coherent professional focus: applied AI systems, especially dependable language-model workflows and learning from evolving data. This connects EvalPath, TrustBench, privacy/security research, and dynamic language modeling. Discuss the defensible field definition with counsel; do not artificially narrow the field to make yourself appear exceptional.

## Days 15-45: substantiate your strongest work

1. Document EvalPath with an employer-confirmed statement of responsibility, dates, what you designed or implemented, decisions you owned, and consequences of the work. Request concrete supporting records rather than generic praise.
2. For each metric, record the baseline, denominator, evaluation period, sample size, method, environment, attribution, and approving source. Prioritize the 35% throughput and 50% documentation/triage figures. Remove or narrow metrics that cannot be reconstructed honestly.
3. Build the same record for SmartProtect. Separate forecast accuracy, overtime, record volume, and staffing utilization rather than implying one establishes the others.
4. Preserve evidence of reviews actually completed. Use permitted acknowledgments; protect confidential manuscripts and reviewer identities.
5. Document your work on coauthored papers through contribution statements, experiments, code history, and coauthor confirmation. Do not upgrade contributing authorship to first authorship.

## Days 46-90: develop useful independent recognition

1. Where ownership permits, make one strongest project or research artifact reproducible: clear README, installation, evaluation commands, data provenance, baselines, limitations, and versioned releases. TrustBench or CoveredAI are candidates, subject to rights and repository review.
2. Collect existing independent uses: meaningful citations, external implementations, evaluations, or documented adoption. Record context instead of relying only on counts or stars.
3. Pursue legitimate work fitting your expertise: substantive peer reviews, technical presentations selected on merit, and collaborations with an actual research question. Record real selection evidence and completed activity.
4. Ask knowledgeable independent experts to explain a specific contribution and its significance with the factual basis for their assessment. Colleagues can document implementation and role; independent experts provide a different perspective where supported.
5. Maintain a dated evidence register and review progress with counsel. Choose filing timing from the strength of the record, not arbitrary quantities of papers, certificates, or reviews.

Do not buy awards, authorship, sham reviews, or flattering coverage to simulate independent recognition. Do not invent adoption, metrics, invitations, or future accomplishments.
